# Candidate Discovery + Winner-Property Study — 2026-10-15

Two questions: (1) what price-behaviour properties do the names that work under LRExtrema
share, and (2) which fresh Nifty 500 names are worth calibrating next.

## Part 1 — What do the winners have in common?

**Design.** Outcome = standalone backtest 2025-01-01 → 2026-10-14 (₹6L / 6.67 % slot, scale-in
off) under three regimes: 15m global config, 4hour and day standard blocks (thr 0.85, stale
10/30 — fixed, not tuned). Properties = 16 daily-candle behaviour measures computed
**point-in-time on calendar 2024**, before the outcome window, so anything that separates
winners is usable for discovery. Two groups, liquidity floor ₹50L/day:
- **A** (40): every name the live bot has traded + watchlist + Sep-27 discovery FITs.
- **B** (34): a seeded random sample of today's scan survivors — the out-of-sample check.

Script: `scripts/property_study.py` + `scripts/property_study_analyse.py`; raw data
`reviews/property_study_20261015.json`.

**Result: no price-behaviour fingerprint survives out of sample.**

| Property (2024) | ρ in A | ρ in B | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| Max drawdown (deeper = more negative) | −0.36 | −0.13 | Same sign, deeper drawdown ↔ better; strong in A, weak in B |
| 5 %-swings per month | +0.15 | +0.21 | Consistent, weak |
| 5-day variance ratio | +0.19 | +0.17 | Consistent, weak |
| Gap frequency (>2 %) | +0.16 | +0.20 | Consistent, weak |
| Median swing size | +0.29 | −0.21 | **Flips** — noise |
| Dip-recovery rate | −0.23 | +0.06 | **Flips** — noise |
| Efficiency ratio, turnover, 2024 return, price, beta | ≈0 | ≈0 | Nothing |

A composite of every property with |ρ| ≥ 0.15 in A, scored on B: ρ = **+0.06 (p 0.74)**. Same
null as the fundamental fingerprint (`project_fingerprint_discovery_null`): stock properties
don't tell you in advance which names the strategy will make money on. "Lively" names (more
swings, deeper prior drawdowns, more gaps) lean slightly better, nothing more.

**What DOES separate them: the timeframe.**

| Regime | Profitable names (all 74) | Mean P&L | Random group B profitable |

blocks are profitable about two-thirds of the time; 15m is worse than a coin flip. Consistent
with the all-15m A/B (2026-09-03) and with live: 6 of the top 8 live earners run on 4hour/day.

treat it as a tie-breaker, never a gate.
