## Setup

- The full production system: `config/config.yaml` as on this branch. That means the 21 watchlist names,
  per-stock params and timeframes, every exit, scale-in, and the 3-session loss block.
- Run through `trader.backtest.engine.run_backtest`, cache-only (no Kite calls). Capital is ₹6L, and
  `max_capital_per_stock_pct` 6.67 gives ₹40k slots at the start.
- **Window: 2015-07-01 → 2026-10-16.** 2011 is impossible because production decides on 15m candles and Kite's 15m
  history starts 2015-02-02. The first months are warm-up.
- **The Kite cache was not touched:**
  - the run used a scratch copy of `data/market.db`;
  - the 15m candles before 2017-12-04 were filled into the copy from the lab store, which comes from the same Kite
    history;
  - the script asserts `config.db_path` points at the copy;
  - the md5 of `data/market.db` was `458a1b277f1f4318ce097745db1117ad` before the copy and is identical after the run.
- Runtime ≈ 7 minutes, 5,373 trade records.

## The early years are flattered by untradeable names

In 2015–2020, several watchlist names were tiny. Their median daily traded value (from the 15m candles):

- TIPSMUSIC: ₹1–13 lakh, at an adjusted price of ₹4–6, where one tick is about 1 %;
- THANGAMAYL: ₹2–60 lakh;
- MAYURUNIQ, CUPID, SCHAEFFLER, GESHIP, ADFFOODS: under ₹4 crore.

A ₹40k order was a large share of those days' trading, and the engine fills at the bar open regardless of volume. So the
P&L from these names in those years is not achievable.

## Caveats (read before using any number)

1. **Hindsight selection.** The watchlist and every per-stock setting were chosen in 2025–26, from names that had
   done well recently. 2021–2026 is partly in-sample. 2015–2020 is out of sample for the settings but not for the
   names: survivors, picked knowing their later run.
2. **Reinvested sizing.** Slots grow with realised P&L: median entry ₹21k in 2015, ₹75k in 2024, ₹148k in 2025.
   Compare years by %, not ₹.
3. **Add-ons stop after 2023.** The share of trades that are add-ons falls from 22 % (2015) to 0 (2024–26). The
   pool is `budget_pct` × the config-file capital, while lots grow with P&L, so add-ons on grown lots no longer fit.
   This is an artefact of a 10-year run with reinvestment, not a behaviour seen live.
4. **Data:** one bad TIPSMUSIC 15m bar (2018-12-14 09:15, open 0) is in the Kite history. CGPOWER's 2016 demerger
   is in the series unadjusted. Neither moves the conclusions.
5. **Not comparable to the campaign FULL baseline (₹439.2k, 2025-01 → 2026-10).** That run starts at ₹6L in
   2025-01. Here 2025 starts with ₹22 L of equity.

## What this says

- The current production system is a 2021-onward winner. On the same names, the same settings would have lost money
  in 2018, 2019 and 2020 (−9 %, −7 %, −2 %), and the 2018 → 2020 slide took 48 % off peak equity. Recovery took 3.4
  years.
- Most of the pre-2021 profit came from names too thin to trade.
- This matches the TD-018 finding that the edge depends on regime and selection, and the stale/loss-block reviews that
  saw the 2025-H1 correction as the hard case. 2018–19, a small/mid-cap bear market, is a harder one that the recent
  windows never tested.
- For the TD-023 sleeve, the same multi-year test (PIT universe, ex-thin names, survivorship-free via TD-022) is the
  bar any lab strategy must clear, and production should be judged on it too.
