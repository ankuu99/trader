## Reading

1. **A01's edge is a ~1-month bounce.** It beats the coins only up to 20 days (19–20/21 at H = 20). At 40–60 days it
   falls to the coin level or below (1/21 at 60, DD 70 %): its deep lows rebound within a month and then fade back.
2. **Production P(min) gets better the longer it is held**: ₹5.7 L → ₹9.3 L → ₹11.6 L → ₹12.3 L → ₹11.5 L for
   5 → 60 days, with Calmar 0.39 and DD 31 % at 60 days, the best risk-adjusted result so far. Ranked across the whole
   universe each day, production's score picks names that keep going for months: a different kind of pick from A01.
3. **Short holds lose to costs for everything**: 5 days on day bars, and one session on 15m (18k trades, −₹14 to
   −₹20 L for every score). A one-week 15m hold also loses.
4. **15m works only as a monthly hold**: A05 at 500 bars (~1 month) makes ₹6.3 L with Calmar 0.36 and DD 31 % —
   it beats all 11 coins on risk-adjusted return and beats production (₹3.95 L / 0.11), though not the coins on raw ₹
   (5/11). The 15m model is useful as an **entry-timing** tool for month-long holds, not as a short-term trader.
5. The two scores look complementary: A01 = month-long bounce, production = multi-month hold. Combining them, or a
   hold set by the model's own forecasts, is the next natural test.
